隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 6 日

概述

小小鸟音乐（英文名 LittleBird，以下简称“本应用”）是一款本地音乐播放器。本应用重视你的隐私，设计上优先采用本地处理方式。

我们处理哪些信息

  • 你主动导入的音乐文件、歌词和封面，用于在设备上播放和展示。
  • 播放进度、收藏、歌单和播放设置，用于提供连续播放和个性化体验。
  • 本应用不会主动收集姓名、通讯录、照片、聊天内容、账号密码、精确位置或支付信息。

数据存储与共享

音乐文件、歌词、封面和播放设置优先保存在你的设备上。本应用不会将这些内容上传到开发者服务器，也不会出售或出租个人信息。除非你主动通过系统分享或向 GitHub 提交 Issue，否则本应用不会主动向第三方发送这些内容。

文件访问权限

只有当你选择“导入音乐”并从系统“文件”中选择内容时，本应用才会读取相应文件。你可以随时在系统设置中管理相关权限。

儿童隐私

本应用不以收集儿童个人信息为目的，也不要求用户创建账号。

政策更新

如果本政策发生变化，我们会在本页面更新生效日期和相关内容。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

Overview

LittleBird (小小鸟音乐) is a local music player. We respect your privacy and design the app to process your content locally whenever possible.

Information we process

  • Music files, lyrics, and artwork that you choose to import, so they can be played and displayed on your device.
  • Playback position, favorites, playlists, and playback settings, so we can provide continuous and personalized playback.
  • We do not actively collect your name, contacts, photos, messages, passwords, precise location, or payment information.

Storage and sharing

Music files, lyrics, artwork, and playback settings are stored on your device by default. We do not upload, sell, or rent this content or personal information. The app does not send this content to third parties unless you choose to share it through the system or submit it in a GitHub issue.

File access

LittleBird reads a file only after you choose “Import Music” and select it from the system Files picker. You can manage access in iOS Settings.

Children’s privacy

The app is not designed to collect children’s personal information and does not require an account.

Updates to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
