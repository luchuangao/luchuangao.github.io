常见问题

如何导入音乐？

打开首页，点击右上角的“+”，选择“导入音乐”，然后从“文件”中选择 MP3、M4A、MP4 等音频文件。导入的文件只保存在你的设备上。

为什么歌词没有显示？

如果歌曲没有歌词，或歌词不包含时间轴，页面会显示“暂无歌词”。带时间轴的歌词才能进行自动高亮和滚动同步。

拖动播放进度后，播放会停止吗？

拖动进度条会定位到新的播放时间，并继续保持当前播放状态。播放页、歌词页、迷你播放器和锁屏控制会同步更新。

如何更换歌曲封面？

在播放页选择歌曲封面后，迷你播放器、锁屏和灵动岛会使用同一张封面图。

支持哪些播放控制？

播放页支持播放、暂停、上一首、下一首和进度调节；锁屏与灵动岛也可以继续控制播放。

Frequently asked questions

How do I import music?

Open Home, tap the “+” button, choose “Import Music,” then select MP3, M4A, MP4, or another supported audio file from Files. Imported files stay on your device.

Why are lyrics not shown?

When a song has no lyrics, or the lyrics do not include timing information, LittleBird shows “No lyrics yet.” Time-synced lyrics are required for automatic highlighting and scrolling.

Will playback stop when I seek?

Dragging the progress bar moves playback to the selected time while keeping the current play state. The player screen, lyrics, mini player, and Lock Screen controls stay in sync.

How do I change artwork?

Choose artwork on the player screen. The mini player, Lock Screen, and Dynamic Island use the same selected artwork.

Which playback controls are supported?

Use play, pause, previous, next, and seek controls on the player screen. Lock Screen and Dynamic Island controls are also supported.
