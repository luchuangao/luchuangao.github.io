Aware Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what information Aware may access, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Last updated: 2026-08-02

1. Information we access

To provide digital wellbeing tools and usage insights, Aware may access the following information:

  • Screen Time data: used to help you understand daily app usage and build healthier digital habits.
  • Device information: such as device model and operating system version, used to improve compatibility and service stability.
  • App usage statistics: used to create your personal usage summaries and reports.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we access only for the following purposes:

  • Showing your digital wellbeing summaries;
  • Helping you reduce unintentional phone use;
  • Improving app features and user experience;
  • Improving app stability and security.

3. Screen Time permissions

Aware uses Apple’s Family Controls and Device Activity APIs to help you manage device usage time.

The related data is managed by Apple’s system permissions and operates under Apple’s platform security mechanisms. We do not read your chats, photos, contacts, passwords, or other private content.

4. Storage and security

Some of your data may be stored only on your device. We use reasonable technical safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration.

5. Information sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties. We will not proactively share your data except when:

  • You have given us clear authorization;
  • Required by applicable laws or regulations;
  • Necessary to protect the safety of users or the public.

6. Third-party services

To improve app stability, we may use third-party services in the future, such as:

  • App crash reporting services;
  • Analytics services;
  • Official Apple services.

These third-party services are subject to their own privacy policies.

7. Your choices and rights

You can at any time:

  • Turn off permissions related to the app;
  • Delete the app and data stored locally;
  • Contact us with privacy-related questions.

8. Children’s privacy

Aware does not knowingly collect children’s personal identity information. If you believe we have unintentionally collected such information, please contact us so we can address it.

9. Policy updates

We may update this policy when product features change or when required by law. Any updated policy will be published on this page.
